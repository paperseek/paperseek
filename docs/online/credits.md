# Service & credits

PaperSeek Service is the default online mode: PaperSeek provides the models, and tasks spend your daily credits instead of your own provider accounts.

## Use PaperSeek Service

1. Sign in. PaperSeek Service is the default mode.
2. Describe your question, choose a data source and a search budget.
3. Start the search. The form shows how many credits the task uses and how many are left today.

Searches and reviews use **Default**, the built-in model chosen by PaperSeek. Preliminary ranking is configured in **Settings › Model service**. Available sources are shown in the application. Web of Science and Wanfang Data have no PaperSeek-provided key: save your own under **Settings › Model service** to search them with PaperSeek Service too.

## Credits

During the public beta every account receives **20 free credits a day**. Only PaperSeek Service uses credits:

| Task | Credits |
| --- | --- |
| Search | 1 |
| Bibliometric analysis | 1 |
| Review | 5 for the whole review |

Credits are deducted when you submit a task. A review is charged once, by its first writing task; generating the outline again, writing, and rewriting sections of the same review are free. **Auto-select by topic** when choosing papers is free and does not count as a writing task. If a task fails or is cancelled, its credits are returned; a task that completes with warnings is not refunded.

Your remaining credits appear in the sidebar and in **Settings › User**, together with the daily refill time.

## Ask for a larger allowance

To ask for a larger allowance during the public beta, email [support@paperseek.app](mailto:support@paperseek.app) with your account ID (copy it in **Settings › User**), your institution, and how you use PaperSeek.
