  • Every stage of the search visible while it runs
  • A score and a written reason for each paper

03Literature reviewExperimental

From evidence to a draft you can check.

Choose the papers. PaperSeek quotes evidence from each abstract, proposes an outline for you to confirm, then writes section by section. Every citation leads back to the sentence it came from.

  • Evidence quoted from each abstract and checked against it
  • An outline you review before any writing starts
  • Export to Word, Markdown or BibTeX
